Why choose N.E.A.T

Funding backed by knowledge and care

  1. Built for the informal sector

    We believe getting the African economy right means focusing on the informal sector, and on hardworking people who need access to funds.

  2. Funding with technical support

    Beyond funding, we give clients the basic knowledge required to keep and improve their businesses.

  3. Evaluation and peer review

    We periodically evaluate clients’ progress and compare them with their peers to foster healthy competition.

  4. Small, simple and low-cost

    We keep overheads low and operations simple so our funds improve lives without putting the burden on clients.
