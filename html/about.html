About N.E.A.T Microcredit

We Are Moving Africa Forward

Who we are, why we exist and the people behind N.E.A.T Microcredit.

Our story

Born from lived experience

It is born out of a series of personal childhood experiences of myself which I hold dear and have planned all my life to remedy.

All the steps I have taken from age 9 till today have been in preparation, planning and execution of this life purpose, which is to ensure that as many as possible do not go through what my mother went through. To ensure that hardworking people with integrity have access to funds and knowledge needed to take their businesses to the next level. The story of how my mother always had to sell her chickens for N40, when she could have gotten as much as N140 for the same chickens if she had access to credit at that time stays in my memories.

— N.E.A.T Philosophy

N.E.A.T Philosophy

How we think about growth

01. Profitability

We understand that the solution to the African problem cannot be found in charity and donations. We must build companies that can solve Africa’s problems at a profit. N.E.A.T Microcredit has been in business since 2017, and we have been profitable since then. This has been achieved by watching our cost closely and keeping our overhead very low without jeopardizing quality customer experience and staff welfare. Based on the experience of our investors over the years, we have given nothing less than 30% per annum on the funds invested with us. That makes it certain that our investors will make back their investment through profit sharing within 3 years. Our members/investors can receive returns on their investment on a monthly, quarterly, biannually or annual basis.

02. Growth Plan

N.E.A.T Microcredit is poised to become the leading financial and investment institution in Africa. Our goal is to support the industrialization of our continent. We are starting at a microcredit level because we understand that we must pull more of our population out of extreme poverty and create strong middle-class families, as that will be required to support our industrialization plan. Therefore, 50% of all our profit are reinvested into the business to ensure accelerated growth and make funds available for more investment.
